21 · Toasts that dismiss themselves ★★☆ extra

concepts: setTimeout · one timer per item · delegation · exercises/21-toasts.html

Notifications that appear, stack up, and take themselves away again.

  • each button shows a toast carrying its own data-message
  • a toast is <li class="toast KIND"><span class="toast-text">…</span><button class="dismiss">×</button></li> — kind is info or error
  • the newest toast goes on top of #toasts
  • every toast removes itself TOAST_MS after it appeared
  • the × button removes one immediately, and cancels its timer

hint: one timer per toast. A single let timer in the outer scope is overwritten by the second toast, so the first one never leaves and clearTimeout can no longer reach it. Keep the ids in a Map keyed by the element, and use one delegated listener on the list — the toasts do not exist yet when you wire it up.
