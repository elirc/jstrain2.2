29 · Reorder a queue with buttons ★★☆ extra

concepts: move the data · delegation · dataset is strings · exercises/29-reorder-list.html

A playlist you reorder with buttons. Buttons, not drag-and-drop — they work with a keyboard, a screen reader, and a phone, which is more than a drag handle manages.

  • render TRACKS into #queue, each row <li class="track"> with a .title and two buttons carrying data-action="up" / "down"
  • Up on the first row and Down on the last are disabled
  • a click swaps the track with its neighbour in the array, then rerenders
  • #queue-order lists the titles joined with ", "

hint: one delegated listener on the list, because every rerender throws the buttons away. Store the row's position in data-index and remember that dataset hands back strings — Number(...) it, or your index arithmetic turns into "2" + 1 === "21".
