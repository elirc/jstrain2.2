33 · Star rating, mouse or keyboard ★★☆ extra

concepts: aria-checked · roving tabindex · clamping · exercises/33-star-rating.html

Five stars. Everything a mouse can do here, a keyboard can do too.

  • a rating of n fills the first n stars: the class on and the character ★, the rest ☆
  • clicking a star sets the rating to its data-value
  • → and ↑ add one, ← and ↓ take one away; the value is clamped between 1 and 5
  • aria-checked is "true" on the chosen star and "false" on every other one
  • roving tabindex: the chosen star is 0 and the rest are -1. With no rating yet, the first star is the 0
  • #rating-value reads 3 of 5, or not rated

hint: the rating is a number and paint() writes every star from it — classes, characters and both attributes. Counting .on elements to find the current value is the version that breaks. Keyboard handling lives on the group, not on each star.
