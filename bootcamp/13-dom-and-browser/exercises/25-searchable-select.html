25 · A select you can type into ★★☆ extra

concepts: filtering · Enter picks · clear before render · exercises/25-searchable-select.html

A dropdown that filters itself while you type — the pattern every "choose a country" field wants and almost none get right.

  • on input, show the CITIES that contain what was typed, case-insensitively
  • each match is <li class="option" data-value="Oslo">Oslo</li> inside #combo-list
  • an empty box or no matches: the list is hidden. No matches also shows #combo-empty
  • Enter picks the first match; a click picks the one clicked. Picking writes the name into #combo-chosen and into the input, and closes the list
  • Escape closes the list and picks nothing

hint: list.textContent = '' before you append, or the third keystroke leaves you with three generations of options. Read "the first match" from the list you just rendered, and call preventDefault() on Enter — inside a real form it would submit the page out from under you.
