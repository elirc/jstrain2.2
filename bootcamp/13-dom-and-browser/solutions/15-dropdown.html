15 · Dropdown menu — SOLUTION ★★☆ core

concepts: components · outside click · stopPropagation · solutions/15-dropdown.html

A menu button with three choices.

  • #menu-btn opens the menu (#menu stops being hidden) and closes it again when clicked a second time; aria-expanded follows
  • clicking a .menu-item sets #chosen to Sorted by: Newest and closes the menu
  • a click anywhere else in the document closes the menu
  • Escape closes the menu

The trap: the click that opens the menu also travels up to your document listener, which promptly closes it again. The menu never appears. Fix it by asking whether the click came from inside the dropdown — #sorter.contains(event.target) — rather than reaching for stopPropagation(), which breaks unrelated listeners on the page.

hint: one setOpen(open) again. The outside-click listener goes on document; the first thing it does is return early for clicks inside #sorter.

Sorted by: none

Anywhere out here counts as "outside".
