24 · Sort a table by its headers — SOLUTION ★★☆ extra

concepts: sort without mutating · aria-sort · rerender · solutions/24-sortable-table.html

Click a header, sort the table. Click it again, sort it the other way.

  • render ROWS into #stock-body — three <td> per row, in the header order
  • a click on a header sorts ascending by its data-key; a click on the header already sorting flips to descending
  • the sorting header gets aria-sort="ascending" or "descending", the class sorted, and ▲ / ▼ in its .arrow. Every other header goes back to none with an empty arrow
  • #sort-state reads price descending, or unsorted before the first click

hint: two traps. [189, 25].sort() compares strings and puts 189 first, so numbers need (a, b) => a - b. And sort() rearranges the array in place — sort a copy (ROWS.slice()) or your source data is scrambled for good.
