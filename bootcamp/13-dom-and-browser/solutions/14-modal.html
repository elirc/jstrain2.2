concepts: components · backdrop click · Escape · solutions/14-modal.html

A modal: a panel over a backdrop that blocks the page.

  • #open-modal shows it — #backdrop stops being hidden, <body> gets the class modal-open, and #modal-state reads open
  • #close-modal hides it again and reverses all of that (#modal-state reads closed)
  • clicking the backdrop itself closes it
  • clicking inside the dialog does not close it
  • Escape anywhere on the page closes it

hint: the backdrop click and the dialog click are the same bubbling event — tell them apart with if (event.target === backdrop). event.target is what was clicked; event.currentTarget is what the listener is on. Escape is a keydown listener on document.
