27 · A form in three steps — SOLUTION ★★★ stretch

concepts: step state · per-step validation · preventDefault · solutions/27-form-wizard.html

Three steps, one form, and no way to reach step 3 with rubbish in step 1.

  • one .step fieldset is visible at a time; #step-label reads Step 2 of 3
  • Next validates the step it is leaving. Step 1 needs a non-blank name, step 2 needs an address containing @. A failure writes into #err-name / #err-email and goes nowhere; success clears the message and advances
  • Back never validates, and is disabled on step 1. Values stay put when you move around
  • step 3 shows name · email in #summary, hides Next and shows Finish
  • Finish submits: preventDefault(), then Sent in #w-done

hint: the state is one number. render() decides what is hidden, what is disabled, and what the summary says — recompute the summary every render and going back to fix a typo just works. Note that a <button> in a form is a submit button unless you write type="button", which is why Next and Back say so.
