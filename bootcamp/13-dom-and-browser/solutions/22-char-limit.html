22 · Counter with a hard limit — SOLUTION ★★☆ extra

concepts: input event · classList.toggle(name, cond) · clamping · solutions/22-char-limit.html

A bio field that counts characters and refuses to go over the limit.

  • #bio-count reads 12 / 40 — used, then LIMIT
  • from WARN_AT characters on, the counter gets the class warn
  • at exactly LIMIT it is full instead — never both
  • text longer than LIMIT is cut back to LIMIT, not merely complained about
  • #save-bio is disabled while the field is blank, and writes Saved: … (trimmed) into #bio-status

hint: one paint() that derives everything from field.value, called on every input event and once at setup. Counting keystrokes yourself breaks on paste, backspace and Ctrl+Z — and classList.toggle('warn') with one argument flips, so a second paint of the same state turns it back off.
