Rapid-fire recall. Answers are hidden behind `<details>` — **say your answer
out loud before you reveal it.** Recall beats recognition; if you hedge, you
got it wrong.

15 questions each, 180 total. Numbering matches the module slots in
[`../docs/ROADMAP.md`](../docs/ROADMAP.md); one quiz can cover more than one
module where the material is continuous.

Do a quiz the day *after* the module, not the same evening — the delay is what
makes it retrieval practice rather than re-reading.
